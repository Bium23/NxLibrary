## Product decision

Adopted for hotfix 0.1.4.1 because it fixes what Ocarina of Time players see today: the dotted rows across fog and light (13b432b4, upstream issue #1537; PR #1544 was closed for it), holes in the ground (e2b76d4e, b547f1e2), Kokiri Forest's white blotches and whiteout through the game's own upscaler (d365496d, 16776913) and the Poe Sisters' and Phantom Ganon's painting colours (3ccb7da0).

## Product decision

The upgrade brings fixes and profiles for games in this library (Super Mario Odyssey, Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Donkey Kong Bananza), new Switch 2 profiles, provisional profiles for NUP files and extracted folders the engine does not know yet, a Home-button quit on Mac, and a build that no longer fails when only its pipeline archive cannot be made. The shader chain changed in 19 commits, so every game rebuilds its shader libraries at its next build; existing apps keep their own runtime until rebuilt. Upstream measurements are specific to their machines and routes; they are not performance results for games tested here.

## Adoption and rollback

The upgrade does not rebuild installed games automatically. Rollback uses the previous pin and engine checkout. A game rebuilt with this engine and launched may have renamed saves with backslash paths, which an older build of that game cannot read.

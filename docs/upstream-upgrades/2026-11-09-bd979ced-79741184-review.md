**Status: adopted for 0.1.4.2 at the maintainer's explicit direction on 2026-11-09.** Previous pin: `bd979ced3ae6bad60bf86429a71d172297d93471`. New pin: `79741184af1558fa1ada0f61741eeecbd615b3f6` (upstream main fetched again immediately before adoption), **71 commits, 254 files**. ## Why adopt this engine

Ocarina of Time benefits across build, startup and play: fewer first-draw stalls (task/mesh pipelines prewarmed and archived), archived-pipeline lookups indexed, compaction limited by tier instead of repeatedly rebuilding the whole archive, large bundled archives mapped instead of copied into RAM, render-target heaps reused as resolution changes, fixes for mist/depth/light-probe artifacts, and safer mesh dispatches above 1,024 workgroups. Frame-step changes target slow motion under load, separately from FPS. Shader arithmetic and HMMA lowering improve translation/GPU work.

Upstream measurements are evidence for investigating, **not measurements or promises for this Mac**. Examples: one HMMA rewrite phase fell from 932 CPU seconds to 3.5 with identical output (1b9775a0; not total build duration); one external-drive Ocarina footprint fell from 19.0 to 12.2 GB (a1e155a3); distinct repeated-start scenarios fell from 762 seconds to 3.3 (4a815619) or 713 seconds to 23.6 and later 2.4 (4c3a03af). Those scenarios, devices, caches and routes differ.

Other upstream results include The Rogue Prince of Persia on Mac (60/up to 120 FPS), OCTOPATH TRAVELER at 60, Minecraft Dungeons II at 60/up to 120, Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity v458752 at 60, and fixes affecting Luigi's Mansion 3, Super Mario Odyssey, Sparking! ZERO, Red Dead Redemption, LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga, Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave, Aokana, Minecraft, and multi-program Metal Gear Solid DLC. They require rebuilt game apps and are not all tested by the maintainer.

## Limits and tradeoffs to keep visible

- The slow-motion frame-step fix is reasoned from the affected games and code, but the upstream note explicitly leaves a before/after run on a sufficiently slow Mac open. Validate actual game speed, not only the FPS counter.
- OCTOPATH TRAVELER's upstream 60 FPS route has more CPU-side long frames than the 30 FPS route; the new cap is not a claim that stutter is resolved. Unity 120 FPS cases also depend on display/vsync behavior.
- BLE controller validation differs by device: Joy-Con 2 motion/pairing/dual/single have upstream physical checks; Pro Controller 2 gyro scale/axes are borrowed and its rumble was not physically certified in the referenced note. Later mouse-sensor support supersedes playing.md's earlier “not yet” statement. No local physical controller test has been performed here.
- Upstream states that a newly signed game build asks for Bluetooth access again. Our icon writer also re-signs the bundle; a repeated Bluetooth permission prompt after an icon change is therefore a possible interaction to verify, not a separately verified macOS guarantee.
- A chain change means a longer next rebuild even with the existing cache. Extraction/workspace data are retained; upstream may replace/prune obsolete generated chain libraries as part of its own normal migration. No manual game-cache deletion was performed.
